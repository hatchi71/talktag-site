# Vocal Camp cumulative speaking pauses

Approved 2026-10-17. Applies to English, Korean, and Japanese Vocal Camp.

- Play each complete cumulative passage before the learner's speaking pause: line 1; then lines 1–2; then lines 1–3; and so on.
- Keep only natural short spacing inside a passage (target 0.35 seconds; preserve existing source tails and leads).
- After the final line, leave `max(3 seconds, complete passage duration × selected multiplier)` of silence. Passage duration runs from the first speech onset to the final speech offset, including natural internal spacing.
- Offer 2.0×, 2.5×, 3.0×, and 3.5×. Keep the recorded voice at its natural speed.
- Show the speaking-pause state only after the full passage, never at internal sentence boundaries. Do not add a second timer after a file that already contains the full pause.
- Preserve scripts, original line recordings, card IDs, repetition targets, progress, completion, and undo.
- Decode and measure all rebuilt variants before publishing. Verify immutable public audio URLs before switching manifests; preserve original news publication ordering for these QC replacements.

This is distinct from Guided L&R's sentence-by-sentence repetition. It does not add pause controls to Essays & Articles.
