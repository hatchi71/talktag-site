2. Read

I usually take the same road to work because it is familiar, but last Monday there was heavy traffic, so I tried a smaller road through a nearby neighborhood. It took a few minutes longer, but the drive was much quieter. I noticed a park and a small bakery that I had never seen before.

Since then, I have taken the new route several times. It is not always faster, but I arrive feeling less stressed. The experience reminded me that the most familiar choice is not always the best one. Sometimes a small change in our routine helps us notice things we normally miss.
