A1 · READABLE 001

My Morning

Build a very short story. Read each line aloud and keep adding one new idea.

A1Daily LifeSnowball → Read → Recall → Talk

1. Snowball

STEP 1

I wake up at seven.

STEP 2

I wake up at seven. I drink some water.

STEP 3

I wake up at seven. I drink some water. Then I wash my face.

STEP 4

I wake up at seven. I drink some water. Then I wash my face. I make some toast.

STEP 5

I wake up at seven. I drink some water. Then I wash my face. I make some toast and eat breakfast. Now I am ready for my day.

2. Read

I wake up at seven. I drink some water and wash my face. Then I make some toast and eat breakfast. I look outside and check the weather. After breakfast, I put on my shoes and leave home. Now I am ready for my day.

3. Recall → Talk

Recall

What time does the speaker wake up?

Retrieve

What does the speaker do before breakfast?

Talk

What do you do first in the morning?
