# Korean-learning audio speaker balance

Approved 2026-10-26. Applies to new Korean-language speech for the Korean learning space: Sound Check (Guided L&R and Audio Essays & Articles), Starter, Vocal Camp, and any future voiced Korean lesson. It does not apply to English or Japanese learning audio, support-language translations, interface narration, or text-only Story Camp and Readable chapters.

## Casting target

- Cast male and female Korean-speaking voices in a **50:50 ratio** across each new production batch. Plan dialogue turns and single-narrator cards together; alternate narrators across monologues rather than defaulting a whole track to one voice.
- Count each distinct Korean spoken line or continuous passage **once**. Repeated drill cycles, pause-length variants, split-card copies of the same source, silence, and start/end dings do not create new speaker assignments.
- Use the approved voice roster's recorded gender metadata. Do not infer a speaker's gender from a name, illustration, character role, or accent. Preserve natural Korean register and scene continuity; do not swap voices mid-character solely to force a per-card split.
- An odd number of indivisible speaking assignments may not divide exactly. Record the difference and give the underrepresented voice group first consideration in the next batch. The rolling Korean-space portfolio target remains 50:50; do not silently relabel an uneven batch as balanced.
- Avoid assigning service, authority, learner, or family roles by gender by default. Balance voice exposure without making the lesson situation less natural.

## Release record and check

For each batch, note the card IDs, approved speaker names/voice IDs, male/female metadata, each speaker's unique source lines or passages, and the resulting counts. Also compare the source-speech duration by speaker so a 50:50 line count does not conceal a large listening-time imbalance. If metadata or duration is unavailable, mark the balance check incomplete and resolve it before claiming that the batch meets this standard.

After audio is generated, retain the normal track-specific technical QC, script alignment, media integrity, public playback, and publication checks. A separate human listening check remains optional under the existing audio policy. This rule does not authorize regenerating, deleting, or changing completion state for published cards; any later revoice follows the ordinary replacement and QC workflow.
