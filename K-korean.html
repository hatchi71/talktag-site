LANGUAGE TRAINING · GUIDED LANGUAGE EXCHANGE

Learn the language. Bring it into a real conversation.

TalkTag has two core programs. Language Training helps you turn Korean you recognize into Korean you can say: listen, repeat, build a story, recall it, and express it in your own words.

At GLEP (Guided Language Exchange Program), we believe in creating a learning environment where Korean and English learners learn from and contribute to one another.

Rather than having one person simply teach and the other learn, both participants take an active role in each other’s language-learning journey. Through their shared efforts to communicate in each other’s languages, they gradually develop a deeper understanding of each other’s language, culture, and perspectives.

Location & Schedule

GLEP sessions are currently held every Saturday and Sunday at Ilgongjae Study Café in Uijeongbu, conveniently located for participants from northern Seoul and nearby areas, including Uijeongbu, Yangju, Dongducheon, and Pocheon.
