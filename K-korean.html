LANGUAGE TRAINING · GUIDED LANGUAGE EXCHANGE

Learn Korean. Meet people. Make it yours.

TalkTag connects online language training with in-person Korean–English exchange. Learn with English guidance, build confidence through practice, and bring your Korean into real conversations.

Two programs. One connected learning experience.

Language Training helps you turn Korean you recognize into Korean you can say. Listen and repeat, take notes as you listen, build stories, and practice recalling and retelling them in your own words.

GLEP—the Guided Language Exchange Program brings Korean and English learners together for guided conversation. Both people learn and contribute. You practice your Korean, help your partner with English, and explore each other’s language, culture, and perspectives.

What you can do at TalkTag

  • Train online: Practice listening, speaking, and retelling at your own pace, with English guidance.
  • Exchange languages: Take turns using Korean and English, asking questions, and helping each other express ideas.
  • Meet in person: Put the expressions you’ve practiced into real conversations at our regular gatherings in Uijeongbu.

Location & Schedule

GLEP sessions are held every Saturday and Sunday at Ilgongjae Study Café in Uijeongbu. We welcome participants from northern Seoul and nearby areas, including Yangju, Dongducheon, and Pocheon.

Prepare online, use the language with another person, then bring what you discover back into practice. That cycle is the heart of TalkTag.

Two languages. Two learners. Two guides. One shared journey.
