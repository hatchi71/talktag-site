# TalkTag release conventions

Before generating or revising any TalkTag audio or reading script, read the complete approved `TALKTAG_DRILLS_FRAMEWORK.md`, then the assigned local reference and track-specific protocol. For English and Korean authoring, use the private Markdown companion in the local reference folder, not the archived PDF; repair any missing or garbled needed section before drafting. Keep reference passages private and write original lesson text. New English and Korean Readable chapters must also follow `READABLE_CHAPTER_PROTOCOL.md`, including source-rights review and the five-step learner path. This standard does not republish old cards or activate unfinished AI features by itself.

For any future English, Japanese, or Korean Level 0 content, also read `LEVEL_0_PRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md` before authoring. Label this pre-A1 pathway `Start Here · Level 0` and preserve its shared listen → repeat → tiny-prompt response structure, language-specific entry points, optional script/translation help, and supported bridge into A1. Do not make alphabet, Hangul, kana, or kanji mastery a prerequisite for speaking. This protocol adoption does not itself create or publish Level 0 cards.

Learner-facing language convention: English and Japanese courses serve Korean-speaking learners, so Spaces destination labels and Japanese lesson titles use Korean. Korean courses serve English-speaking learners, so guidance and Spaces destination labels remain English. Preserve the language selector labels ENGLISH / KOREAN / 日本語. Do not globally translate all three audiences' interfaces into Korean.

Home bulletin board: exactly three fixed slots. Slot 1 is the current Talk & Tag gathering notice; slots 2 and 3 show the newest two published content groups, newest first. Run `node scripts/update-home-news.mjs` for every content upload/publication, or install `.githooks` with `git config core.hooksPath .githooks`. The pre-commit hook rebuilds the feed automatically. Do not manually edit homepage upload notices. Preserve first-publication dates for QC replacements, translations, splits and styling changes. New catalog modules must be supported by the generator. Never include drafts or unavailable audio.

For R2 uploads, read `R2_UPLOAD_PROTOCOL.md`. If the browser uploader fails, use a user-authorized, short-lived bucket-scoped S3 token and verify local, R2 and public SHA-256 before exposing media. Revoke the token after live checks; never store bucket write credentials in the repository.

For every Guided Listen & Repeat upload, replacement or deployment, read and apply `GUIDED_AUDIO_PROTOCOL.md`. Episode recordings over 10 minutes must become independently selectable sentence-boundary parts, with an 11-minute hard maximum per card. Preserve full repetition cycles, English/Korean synchronization and original episode start/ending cues. Run `node scripts/validate-guided-cards.mjs` before publication. Do not apply this splitting rule to other tracks.

Before drafting any new Guided L&R script, locate and read the assigned source in the local `0. Reference Materials` folder. Use the assigned material as level/topic guidance, not as copy: write original dialogue and independently written Korean support. If no material is assigned or the relevant section cannot be identified, pause script drafting and ask which reference to use.

Apply level-specific content: A1/A2 focus on basic expressions and vocabulary from the assigned references; B1/B2 use practical situations and increasingly complex, well-structured sentences; C1/C2 use natural real-life American or British English, including practical phrasal verbs and intellectually engaging language without sounding artificial. Match the chosen speaker accent to the script.

Learner-facing pages must retain TalkTag visual consistency and pass mobile 360/375/390/430 CSS px, Bright/Dark and desktop checks. Preserve 44px touch targets, safe areas, no horizontal overflow, and manual completion/undo. Do not publish administrator prototypes or unrelated unfinished work.

Shared five-category level-picker standard, approved 2026-10-26: English, Korean and Japanese Guided Listen & Repeat, Audio Essays & Articles, Vocal Camp, Story Camp and Readable use the same level-first presentation. Put a clear track title and short purpose before a “Choose your level” heading and total completion summary. Use generous A1/A2, B1/B2 and C1/C2 cards in two columns on phones, with each level's completion count and progress bar. A full-width “Start Here · Level 0” card appears only where Level 0 content is actually published; do not imply unavailable lessons are ready. Place a compact, initially collapsed “Completed missions” shelf immediately below the level cards, before the practice guide or explanatory material. Preserve language-specific learner-facing labels, existing level and lesson routes, completion records, reopen and undo behavior. Treat the referenced Guided L&R screenshot as the visual model, including light/dark treatment, 360–430px mobile checks and desktop balance. This standard changes presentation, not learning content or publication status.

Guided L&R ding update, approved 2026-10-09: each independently selectable audio card must contain its own start and ending dings, including split episode parts. This supersedes first-part/last-part-only dings. Preserve full sentence cycles, quality, bilingual synchronization and duration limits.

Audio listening-check update, approved 2026-10-25: a separate human listening or pronunciation check is optional, not a universal publication gate. Run the required track-specific technical QC, alignment, timing, integrity, and public-playback checks; never claim human listening occurred if it did not. See `GUIDED_AUDIO_PROTOCOL.md` and `MINISKIT_AUDIO_PROTOCOL.md` for unresolved-defect handling.

Korean-learning audio speaker balance, approved 2026-10-26: target **50% male and 50% female voices** across each new Korean-space production batch. Before casting or replacing Korean speech, read `KOREAN_SPEAKER_BALANCE_PROTOCOL.md`; record the planned speakers and verify the balance on unique spoken source material, not repetitions, silence, dings, or encoded variants. Apply this across Korean Sound Check, Starter, Vocal Camp, and any other Korean-language audio track. Do not recast published audio merely to adopt the rule; replacements still require their normal QC and release checks. This does not change the English or Japanese voice policies.

GLR update approved 2026-10-10: A1/A2 10 repetitions, B1/B2 15, C1/C2 21. New C1/C2 finished cards prefer under 15 minutes; existing longer C cards are exempt from the old 10/11-minute cap. A/B duration rules remain unchanged. Preserve scripts, dings and completed states when increasing only repetition counts; use revision-specific resume positions when audio timing changes.

Mini Talks direction, approved 2026-10-27: replace Audio Essays & Articles with short everyday spoken skits in Sound Check across English, Korean, and Japanese. Use the shared Mini Talks label and listen-and-repeat interaction; do not relabel or publish old essay recordings as conversations. Speaker counts increase flexibly with level; the American-majority and B2–C2 accent-diversity guidance applies to English only. Read `MINI_TALKS_PRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md` before authoring or migrating cards, and keep the old learner catalog intact until verified replacement audio is ready. In the five-category level-picker standard above, read Mini Talks wherever it still says Audio Essays & Articles.

TalkTag learning promise, stated 2026-10-27: **“Step by step, into real conversation.”** For the Korean course: **“Listen, speak, read, and make Korean your own—one small piece at a time.”** Adapt only the target-language name for English, Japanese, and future courses. Judge each new lesson by whether its small, supported steps move the learner toward independently using the language in real conversation; do not treat completing a drill as the end goal. This is production guidance, not a request to replace existing published page copy.

Content quality standard, reaffirmed 2026-10-27: preserve the strengths of the published Mini Talks 001 scripts and voice characters in future production. Apply the same quality policy across English, Korean, Japanese, and future TalkTag language areas: begin with a concrete, useful real-life situation; write level-appropriate lines people would naturally say to one another rather than textbook exposition; cast and direct believable voice characters whose delivery fits their relationship and purpose; and check that learners can use or adapt the language outside the lesson. Use the same production sequence in every language area—approved framework and assigned reference, original target-language script and aligned meanings, character-based casting when audio is involved, track-specific translation/audio QC, then verified publication. Author naturally in each target language rather than literally translating one language's dialogue. Keep language-specific pronunciation, accent, speaker-balance, learning-purpose, and technical rules in force.

Audio production rule, approved 2026-10-27: cast character voices and gender from the script's actual story roles, not a per-card quota; keep the Korean-space 50:50 production-batch target only where it can be met naturally. Interpret the requested “16db” as a −16 LUFS integrated target for the **finished encoded audio**, with true peak no higher than −1 dBTP. Measure and record both values in TalkTag Audio QC Studio; do not apply a fixed 16 dB gain. This rule applies to newly produced or replaced speech audio in every language area and track; it does not silently reprocess published files or weaken track-specific alignment, timing, translation, and release checks.
