# English Readable 001 · A1–C2 production record

## Shared source and rights

- Source: Beatrix Potter, *The Tale of Peter Rabbit*, Frederick Warne & Co., 1902, from Peter's departure for Mr. McGregor's garden through his return home ([Project Gutenberg eBook 14838](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14838)). The selected portion is the complete garden-and-return episode, not a modern illustrated edition.
- Source language / original rights holder: English / Beatrix Potter (died 1943). The original work is public domain in the US and past the standard life-plus-70 term in the UK and Korea. The detailed US/UK/Korea evidence and limits are recorded in `en-a1-001-authoring.md`. This decision does not cover modern translations, adaptations, trademarks, or illustrations.
- Intended uses: six independently written level adaptations and six newly written Korean support translations on the public, potentially commercial TalkTag site. No original prose or illustrations are reproduced verbatim. Each chapter identifies Potter and the 1902 work and links to the source.
- The level versions share the event arc but not identical prose. Adapted interpretive language is TalkTag's own; it is not presented as Potter's quotation or as a scholarly account of her intent.

The protected Markdown references were read privately for level, genre and structure; they are not publishable source texts. The OCR-assisted pages used for A1/B1 and B2 were checked for readable sequence, and no wording was copied. The English source episode was checked against the 1902 text. Each Korean paragraph was written to express its paired English meaning naturally, not word-for-word.

The learner input is a complete target-language chapter with opt-in Korean support. Expected output progresses from concrete recall to analysis of motive and narrative stance. All levels retain the five-step Read → Recall → Revise → Retell → Discuss pathway, with the chapter hidden during recall, original draft preserved, oral retell encouraged, and AI discussion honestly marked coming soon. No learner writing is sent to an AI service.
