## Vocal Camp · A1–C2

- Concrete situation: two groups arrive for the same booked classroom; a teacher and front-desk coordinator resolve the conflict.
- Learner input: one original US-English spoken meaning unit at a time; each cumulative stage restarts at unit 1. A1–B2 have three stages, C1–C2 five.
- Expected output: speak the entire accumulated event during the post-passage pause, then close the model and tell a similar real event with a changed person, place, or outcome.
- Physical: see the occupied room, booking message, and adjacent available room; ask the desk for help.
- Sound: hear and say the whole expanding account with natural spacing, then speak during a 2.0–3.5× pause (2.5× default).
- Graphic: one room-door/booking-board illustration plus an optional aligned English/Korean script after the first hearing.
- Meta: explain how to check a booking and propose an alternative without blaming either group.

## Story Camp · A1–C2

- Concrete situation: a community class finds its room occupied and needs a practical, courteous solution.
- Learner input: original English writing units shown cumulatively; A1–B2 have five, C1–C2 seven. The Korean meaning is support, not the target text.
- Expected output: hide the complete model and reconstruct it in writing, revise the sequence, retell it aloud, and write a plausible version with a different choice or result.
- Physical: door, two groups, booking record, available room.
- Sound: retell the finished story to another person after written reconstruction.
- Graphic: the same scenario illustration and the accumulated English story, with Korean meaning available on demand.
- Meta: distinguish the apparent conflict from the scheduling error, then decide how to prevent recurrence.

Level progression: A1 names people, room, and outcome; A2 adds a routine schedule check; B1 connects cause and practical action; B2 adds tact and perspective; C1 and C2 separate evidence, assumptions, and durable resolution. Each unit is a brief beat, not a paragraph. No TOEIC task or factual institutional claim is implied.
