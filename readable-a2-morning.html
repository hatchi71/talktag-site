A Small Change
in My Morning

Build the first paragraph one piece at a time. Then read the complete story and try to bring the idea back without looking.

A2Daily LifeSnowball → Read → Recall → Talk

1. Snowball

Read each step aloud. Keep the earlier words and add the new part.

Step 1

I usually wake up at seven.

Step 2

I usually wake up at seven, but I often feel tired.

Step 3

I usually wake up at seven, but I often feel tired, so I started going to bed earlier.

Step 4

I usually wake up at seven, but I often feel tired, so I started going to bed earlier. Now I put my phone away at ten.

Step 5

I usually wake up at seven, but I often feel tired, so I started going to bed earlier. Now I put my phone away at ten and read a book for about twenty minutes.

Step 6

I usually wake up at seven, but I often feel tired, so I started going to bed earlier. Now I put my phone away at ten and read a book for about twenty minutes. This small change helps me sleep better.

2. Read

Now read the whole text naturally. Focus on the story rather than individual words.

A Small Change in My Morning

I usually wake up at seven, but I often feel tired, so I started going to bed earlier. Now I put my phone away at ten and read a book for about twenty minutes. This small change helps me sleep better.

I still feel tired sometimes, but my mornings are getting easier. I have more time for breakfast, and I do not have to hurry as much. I think small changes can make our daily lives better.

3. Recall → Talk

Close the text or scroll it out of view. Do not try to reproduce every sentence exactly.

Recall

What problem did the speaker have in the morning?

Retrieve

What two things did the speaker change at night?

Talk

What small change could make your own morning better?
